# Atelon License Policy

Atelon displays 3D models made by other people, so every model needs a clear license record.

| License | Decision |
|---|---|
| CC0 | Accept |
| CC BY (4.0 / 3.0) | Accept, with attribution on the Credits page |
| CC BY-SA | Review case by case (share-alike obligations) |
| Editorial-only | Review case by case |
| Royalty-free marketplace | Review case by case; check redistribution limits |
| CC BY-NC | Reject (non-commercial) |
| Unclear, view-only, all rights reserved | Reject unless written permission is obtained |

## Rules
1. Read the license text on the listing page. A price of zero does not grant commercial rights.
2. Every accepted model gets a row in `data/ledger.csv` before it is used.
3. No bulk scraping of Sketchfab, CGTrader, 3D Warehouse or any marketplace. Download manually, one model at a time, logged in with your own account.
4. CC0 does not guarantee manufacturer logos or branding are cleared. Remove or replace logos you are unsure about.
5. Every CC BY model needs an attribution line: `'Model name' by Author, CC BY 4.0, via Sketchfab`.
6. Show the tier honestly: Full 3D, Reference 3D (approximate, labelled) or Specs only.
7. Atelon is independent of all manufacturers. Keep a takedown email on the About page.
8. Get advice from a lawyer or IP clinic before ads, affiliates or payments. This policy is not legal advice.
